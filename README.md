# SquadPick — 7 vs 7 Football Team Balancer ⚽

A specialized web application designed to select two balanced 7-a-side teams (14 players total) with authentic tactical pitch formations, goalkeeper guarantee, and instant WhatsApp team sheet export.

---

## 🌟 7 vs 7 Matchday Features

- **⚽ 7-a-Side Tactical Pitch (14 Players)**: Full 2D football turf displaying 7 players per team in authentic formations:
  - **2 - 3 - 1** (Standard: 1 GK, 2 DEF, 3 MID, 1 ATT)
  - **3 - 2 - 1** (Solid Defense: 1 GK, 3 DEF, 2 MID, 1 ATT)
  - **2 - 2 - 2** (Dual Attack: 1 GK, 2 DEF, 2 MID, 2 ATT)
- **🧤 Goalkeeper Fairness Engine**: Guarantees exactly 1 GK per team so neither side is forced into an unfair outfield keeper situation.
- **⚖️ Star-Rating Balance**: Evaluates skill ratings (1 to 5 stars) across outfield positions to minimize rating disparities.
- **🔄 Interactive Player Swap**: Click any two players on the pitch to swap them immediately between teams.
- **📲 WhatsApp 7v7 Lineup Export**: One-click copy that formats the full match sheet by position (GK, DEF, MID, ATT) with emojis ready to send to your group.
- **⏱️ 20-Minute Half Clock**: Includes synthesized Fox 40 referee whistle for kickoff and half-time.
- **🪙 3D Captains' Coin Toss**: Virtual coin toss for kickoff or side selection.
- **💾 Auto-Save**: Saves all 14 player names, positions, and ratings in localStorage.
